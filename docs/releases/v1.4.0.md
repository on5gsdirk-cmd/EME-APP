# EME APP 1.4.0

Look ahead up to 48 hours on the Moon tab, a message when a new version is out, Chat CQ with each station once, and Czech and Ukrainian.

## New
- Moon tab: a slider under the world map looks up to 48 hours ahead, in steps of 10 minutes. The time, the world map, the moon phase, AZ/EL, the distance and "Moon clear of your obstacles" follow the chosen time, shown in orange with "Ahead: +5:30". The Live CQ stations are hidden on the map while you look ahead, and the card with moonrise, culmination and moonset stays about now. "Now" brings everything back. In the app and in the web app. (#122)
- The app tells you when a new version is out: once a day it asks GitHub, and a bar above the tabs offers the download. Close it to hide it until the next version, or switch the check off in Settings (GitHub sees the IP address of your phone). Only in the app: the web app updates itself. (#127)
- Two new languages: Czech and Ukrainian, now 15 in all. The translations are new; corrections by native speakers are welcome. In the app and in the web app. (#121)

## Changed
- Chat CQ (HB9Q and N0UK): a station that calls CQ several times is shown once, with its most recent CQ. On N0UK the line is greyed out when the station wrote "stop cq" after it. In the app and in the web app. (#125)

## Fixed
- Moon tab: on a small phone with large letters, the time zone after the local time could be cut off in some languages. (#121)

## Updating
- Installs over 1.3.0; settings, your obstacles and the HB9Q login are kept.
